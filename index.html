Embedded · LoRa · INSA 2026

SLF-EWN — Landslide / Flood Early-Warning

End-to-end embedded monitoring system developed as Group 1 lead at the INSA National Cyber Talent Summer Camp. Goal: detect rising subsurface moisture and ground movement before slope failure — sensing near the hazard, communicating without buried cables. Buried ATmega328P nodes wake from low-power sleep, power sensors only while measuring (capacitive moisture + LIS3DH tilt), pack CRC-protected 12-byte frames, and send them through roughly one metre of soil via SX1278 / Ra-02 LoRa to a surface tower. The tower validates frames, stores 16-byte records in W25Q64 SPI flash (continues logging when the PC is offline), and serves data to a Qt 6 / QML desktop dashboard over USB-UART. Production firmware is kept separate from Proteus fake-device firmware so the shared protocol is tested end-to-end before fabrication. One-layer through-hole PCB designs; 3–7 year battery life on Li-SOCl2 is a design target. Status: simulation-stage (working code, design, dashboard, demo video) — no hardware fabricated yet; Phase II still running.
